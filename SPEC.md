Project: Invoice Extractor (web app, installable as PWA)
Who: small finance/accounts teams, shop owners
Problem: manually typing invoice details into spreadsheets
What it does:
- user logs in
- uploads an invoice (photo or PDF)
- app extracts vendor, invoice no., date, line items, total
- uncertain fields are highlighted and editable
- invoice history + CSV export
Inputs (3 ways):
1. Upload photo/PDF → AI extracts fields
2. Paste invoice text → AI extracts fields
3. Manual entry form → user types fields
Done when:
- 30 test invoices: 90%+ of fields correct
- deployed at a public link
- at least 1 real person has tried it
Out of scope: accounting integrations, team accounts, payments
